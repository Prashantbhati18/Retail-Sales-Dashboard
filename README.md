# **Retail Sales Performance Dashboard**

* #### **Overview**

This project presents an interactive Retail Sales Performance Dashboard created in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Timeline, and KPI Cards.

The dashboard helps analyze sales performance across different business dimensions.



* #### **Dashboard Preview**

!\[Dashboard](Dashboard%20Screenshot/dashboard.png)



* #### &#x20;**Features**

\- Total Sales KPI

\- Total Profit KPI

\- Number of Orders

\- Total Quantity Sold

\- Monthly Sales Trend

\- Sales by State (Map Chart)

\- Sales by Segment

\- Sales by Category

\- Top 10 Products

\- Top 10 Customers

\- Interactive Slicers

\- Timeline Filter

\- Business Insights Section



* #### **Dataset**

Sample Superstore Dataset

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets

\- Microsoft Excel

\- Pivot Tables

\- Timeline

\- Map Chart

\- Doughnut Chart

\- Bar Chart

\- Line Chart



* #### **Business Insights**

\- Technology generated the highest sales.

\- Consumer segment contributed over 50% of total sales.

\- California recorded the highest sales among all states.

\- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the top-selling product.

\- November achieved the highest monthly sales.
